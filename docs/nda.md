**Kaar e Khair o Barr**: Member Confidentiality Agreement (v1)

This agreement is between **Kaar e Khair o Barr** ("KKB"), a volunteer welfare initiative based at UET Lahore, Pakistan, and **you**, the member signing below.

You are joining KKB as a volunteer. In your work you may see information about the people we help, the people who support us, and how KKB operates. This agreement explains how you must handle that information. Its purpose is to protect the dignity and privacy of beneficiaries and donors, and the trust people place in KKB.

### 1. What counts as confidential
"Confidential information" means anything you learn through KKB that is not already public. In particular:

1. **Beneficiary information:** names, photos, addresses, phone numbers, family details, medical conditions, financial situation, and any case details of the people and families KKB supports.
2. **Donor and supporter information:** identities, contact details and donation amounts, especially of donors who wish to remain anonymous.
3. **Member information:** other members' personal details, such as phone numbers, emails and addresses.
4. **Financial information:** account details, balances, budgets, receipts and fund allocations.
5. **Internal matters:** unannounced campaigns, events and plans; internal discussions in KKB groups, including WhatsApp groups; meeting notes; and internal documents.
6. **Access details:** passwords, login links, invite codes and access to KKB accounts, files or systems.

### 2. What you agree to do
1. **Use it only for KKB work.** Use confidential information only to carry out tasks for KKB, and only as much as the task needs.
2. **Keep it private.** Do not share it with anyone outside KKB. Within KKB, share it only with members who need it for their work.
3. **Protect beneficiaries' dignity.**
   - Do not photograph, record or post any beneficiary, or any information that could identify them, without KKB's approval and the beneficiary's consent.
   - Never share images of anyone in distress for publicity without that consent.
4. **Don't speak for KKB without permission.** Do not post, publish or make public statements on behalf of KKB, or use KKB's name, logo or social media accounts, unless KKB has authorised it.
5. **Handle money honestly.** Record and hand over any donation you collect in the way KKB directs. Never use it for any other purpose.
6. **Keep access details secure.** Do not share your account, passwords or invite codes. Tell an admin straight away if you think something has been exposed.
7. **Report problems.** If confidential information is shared by mistake, tell a KKB admin as soon as you can.

### 3. What is not covered
This agreement does not stop you from sharing information that:
- was already public, through no fault of yours;
- you already knew before joining KKB, from a source not bound by confidentiality;
- KKB has approved for sharing in writing, including in the app or a KKB group message from an admin; or
- you are required to disclose by law or by a court or government authority. If allowed, tell KKB first.

### 4. When you leave
1. You can ask to leave KKB at any time through the app.
2. When you leave, return or delete any KKB documents, files, photos and data you hold, and leave any KKB-only groups you are asked to leave.
3. Your duty to keep information confidential **continues for two years after you leave**. For personal information about beneficiaries, donors and members, it **continues for as long as the information remains private**.

### 5. Breaking this agreement
If you break this agreement, KKB may:
- suspend or remove you from KKB and its groups; and
- take any other action available to it under the law of Pakistan.

### 6. General
1. **No employment.** Membership in KKB is voluntary and unpaid. This agreement does not create employment, partnership or any right to payment.
2. **Electronic signature.** You sign by ticking the agreement box and typing your full name in the KKB app. KKB stores the version you signed, your typed name and the date and time. You agree that this electronic signature has the same effect as a handwritten one.
3. **Changes.** KKB may update this agreement. You will be asked to sign the new version before continuing to use the app. If you do not agree, you can ask to leave.
4. **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan. The courts at Lahore have jurisdiction.
